## Rules (verified)

IFAB Laws of the Game, Law 4.3 (theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players-equipment): undershirts must be "a single colour which is the same colour as the main colour of the shirt sleeve" or a pattern that exactly replicates the sleeve; "undershorts/tights must be the same colour as the main colour of the shorts or the lowest part of the shorts – players of the same team must wear the same colour." This is the real parent pain point (buying black skins for a team in red sleeves) and answers the "FA kit regulations" PAA. Many grassroots leagues and clubs set their own colour, so the article should tell parents to check with the manager before buying.

Snoods: Law 4 no longer names them (covered by 4.1 dangerous equipment); IFAB banned snoods in matches in 2011. Verify the current position with an FA or IFAB source before stating it in the article.
