# Football Parent — Content Plan (6 Articles), with real research

**Status:** Registered in the article tracker as `planned` (`seo-data/exports/article-tracker.csv`).
**Superseded doc:** replaces the original estimate-only version of this plan (built from GSC-comparable pages only, before DataForSEO research).
**Research run:** 2026-08-09, live UK/English DataForSEO data. Total spend across all calls: ~$0.29.
**Database:** every keyword below is persisted in `seo-data/database/seo.db` (`keywords`/`keyword_metrics`), mapped to its article's `target_url`.

> **Read this before writing any of these articles.** The original volume estimates (triangulated from comparable GSC pages) were directionally wrong for 3 of 5 articles - see each section's "estimate vs actual" note. Real numbers below.

**Updated publish-order suggestion:** Fulham first (confirmed real demand, low competition), then the new **Ball Mastery Drills for 7-8 Year Olds** (article 6 - KD 0, the single best volume-to-competition ratio in this whole batch), then Tottenham, then the broader Football Drills by Age piece (article 4, now that its only real-volume terms have moved to article 6), then 3 and 5 (both have zero measured Google Ads volume but confirmed real search intent via People Also Ask - see caveat below).

> **Why article 6 exists:** the original plan had one "Football Drills for Kids by Age" article. Research showed its generic primary keyword has zero real volume, and the ONLY real volume in that whole cluster was two narrow terms: `football drills for 7 year olds` (140/mo) and `football drills for 8 year olds` (210/mo, high £5.29 CPC), both KD 0. Rather than bury the one proven-demand angle as one H2 among many, it's been split into its own dedicated article. The two keywords were moved off article 4 to avoid both pages targeting the same terms - see each section below.

> **Caveat on the zero-volume articles (3 and 5):** Google Ads' keyword volume tool suppresses conversational/long-tail "how to..." queries below its reporting threshold - it shows 0 even when real searches happen. Real Google "People Also Ask" data (pulled separately, see below) confirms genuine search intent for both topics. Don't read "0" as "no one searches this" - read it as "no exact-match head term captures it; the demand is in the question-shaped long tail."
