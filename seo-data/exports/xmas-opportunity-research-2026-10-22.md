## 3. Gifts guide: what's winnable and how

**Page one is winnable for the "football gifts for kids / boys / [age]" family.** No People Also Ask on the main term, AI Overview citing Amazon, Argos and Smyths. Organic is mostly small blogs and shop round-ups: Red Letter Days, childrensfootball.com, The Soccer Store's 2025 Christmas list, JD Sports blog, footy.com, sportsgearswag, footballflick, juniorballers ("Football Gifts for 10 to 12 Year Olds"), Etsy, eBay, Amazon. Google hides the volume on the "kids" phrasings, but the parent term ("football gifts" / "gifts for footballers") was 22,200 in November and December each. The guide (published 9 Oct) has 570 impressions and 17 clicks so far at 7.4.

**What Google is asking for that the guide doesn't say in those words:**
- Age phrasing: related searches are all "football gifts for 7 / 8 / 10 / 13 year old boy", and PAA asks "What to buy a 7 year old who loves football?" and "What to get a 10 year old boy who loves football?". Our "Football Gifts by Age" section uses Under 7 / Under 9 bands only.
- "secret santa football gifts" 1,300 in Dec (68x), "football gifts under 10" 260: a cheap gifts line or short section.
- Girls: "football gifts for kids girls" and "for 10 year old girl" are related searches.

**Pyjamas, slippers and annuals:** shopping SERPs (M&S, pyjamas.com, Wynsors, Amazon, eBay, Etsy; annuals are bookshops). An article would not rank. They are worth a line each in the guide as affiliate picks, not as SEO targets. "football boot slippers" (2,900 in Nov) is a novelty-slipper search, mostly adult.

**Recommended changes (one update, the page is new and low traffic):**
1. Ages added to the age bands: "Under 7 and Under 8 (ages 5 to 7)" and so on, so the "X year old" searches match. No section renamed.
2. Two FAQs from PAA: "What do you buy a 7 year old who loves football?" and "What do you get a 10 year old who loves football?", answered from the age section.
3. A "Secret Santa and gifts under £10" line in Stocking Fillers.
4. Pyjamas, slippers and the 2027 annuals as one "Bedroom and reading" line in Stocking Fillers, plus the advent calendar link once that article exists.

On watch to about 27 Oct (13 Oct title). Make the change after that.
