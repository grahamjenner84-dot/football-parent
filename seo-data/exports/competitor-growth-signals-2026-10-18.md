## What this says [inference]

- **SubTime grew through the app stores, mostly in the US.** It has been on the stores for nine years, picks up steady reviews and ranks #1 in Play for "substitution app". It has almost no website search and no ads were seen. Store ranking and ratings did the work.
- **TeamStats grew through clubs and leagues, plus search at scale.** Clubs adopt it club-wide ("my club has used this"), its league and team pages earn about 17,700 ranked keywords, and it runs a small, steady Meta campaign. Reviews pick up sharply from 2025, which suggests a recent push (an in-app review prompt is one plausible cause; not proven).
- **Centre Circle grew through county FAs.** Referees and leagues are allocated games through it, 190 county FA pages link to it, and reviews only take off from 2025 as FAs moved onto it. An institutional route, not marketing.
- **CoachAny has simply been on the store since 2015.** Slow and steady, with no sign of a push.
- **None of them grew through people searching for the brand on Google.** All four are below Google Trends' threshold for brand searches in the UK and US.

## What it means for us [inference]

- The store route (SubTime) needs ratings and time; our listing has neither yet.
- The club-adoption route (TeamStats) is the one that compounds in the UK: one club decision brings many coaches and parents. The Coach App's per-team pricing and share links fit it, and a club-level offer would go further.
- The institutional route (Centre Circle) needs a county FA or league relationship. The Somerset FA "Even Stevens" and Sheffield FA equal-playing-time pages rank for our calculator searches and would be the natural first conversations.
