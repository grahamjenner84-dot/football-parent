# Christmas and Black Friday affiliate seasonality, 22 Oct 2026

Google Ads UK monthly volume (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026) for 84 terms, Google Trends weekly series (last 12 months) on 20, and live SERPs on 3. Script `scripts/seo/cli/xmas-affiliate-seasonality.ts`, data `xmas-affiliate-seasonality-2026-10-22.json`. Spend about $0.11. Google hides volume for most "kids ..." phrasings, so Trends stands in for those.

## Timing (Trends, "football gifts")

Late October sits at about a quarter of the peak (Oct 2025: 26/100). Early November roughly doubles (51), Black Friday week (23-30 Nov) and 1-20 December are both at the peak (91 and 90), then it falls off before Christmas. Advent calendar searches are the exception: they peak around 9 November and are gone by December. A new page needs one to two weeks to start getting impressions (the base layers guide reached 3,278 in its first week), so anything for this season should be live by about 7 November.
