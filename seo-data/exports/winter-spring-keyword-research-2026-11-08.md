# Winter and spring keyword research (Dec 2026 to May 2027), 8 Nov 2026

The question: which kids' football searches grow over the next few months, which carry affiliate potential, and which are high-volume and low-difficulty enough to write now.

**Data:** Google Ads Keyword Planner via the `get_keyword_metrics` / `get_keyword_ideas` MCP tools. UK, English, monthly volumes Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, so "Jan" means Jan 2026, the best guide to Jan 2027. About 280 terms were checked across 4 idea pulls and 3 exact-term pulls.

**Difficulty caveat:** no DataForSEO keys were available in this session, so there is **no measured KD**. Difficulty here is read from two things: (a) Keyword Planner's competition index, which measures *ad* competition and not organic difficulty, and (b) a qualitative look at who ranks, using a US-biased web search rather than a UK Google SERP. Treat every "winnable" call as a lead to confirm with a `googleOrganicSerp` + KD pull before writing.

Christmas and Black Friday are already covered (`xmas-affiliate-seasonality-2026-10-22.md`, advent calendars, gifts guides), so this research starts after Christmas.

**Indexing lead time:** new pages have needed 1 to 2 weeks to get impressions, and longer to settle. Each publish-by date below allows about 4 weeks before the rise.

### 1. Football boot dryers and boot care (publish now, peak Nov to Jan)

| Term | Nov | Dec | Jan | Feb | Rest-of-year low |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| boot dryer | 8,100 | 6,600 | 4,400 | 2,900 | 880 (May) |

| football boots dryer | 480 | 320 | 140 | 110 | 20 |
| how to clean / wash football boots | 880 | 480 | 720 | 590 | 320 |
| how to dry football boots | 260 | 90 | 110 | 110 | 30 |

- **Why:** this is the most seasonal affiliate term found that the site has no page for. Football boot dryer is 31x more searched in Nov than in May. Muddy, wet winter pitches are the reason.
- **SERP read:** weak. Results are aggregator review pages, desertcart listings, a forum thread and a hotukdeals deal, and no specialist football site ranks. Likely winnable.
- **Page:** `/football-gear/best-football-boot-dryers` covering how to dry, clean and stop boots smelling, without cracking leather or melting glue. Answer "how to dry football boots" (newspaper, no radiator) above the picks.
- **Affiliate:** electric boot dryers (Sothing, Snowpea, MaxxDry-type), boot deodoriser balls, boot bags, brushes.
- **Internal links in:** boots for kids, AG vs FG, soft ground boots, base layers (already mentions dryers), gifts guide.
- **Timing:** it is already past the start of the peak. Publish this week to catch Dec and Jan; it will be fully placed for next Oct.

- **Why:** the volume is big and steady, with bumps in Sep and Jan as training load rises. Ages 8 to 14 overlap our audience exactly. The site only mentions it in one line, in `how-much-training-is-too-much.mdx`.
- **SERP read:** the head term belongs to NHS trusts, patient.info, Healthline and physio clinics, so it is hard to rank for. The football-parent angle has no strong owner: whether to keep playing, which boots, astro vs grass, and what to tell the coach. Clinic blogs and thin sites rank there. Target the long tail first.

- **Page:** `/football-development/severs-disease-football`, titled "Sever's Disease in Young Footballers: Heel Pain, Boots and When to Play".
- **Affiliate:** gel heel cups, heel raises, boots with a raised heel and moulded studs (a link to the boots guide), and ice packs. Outbound clinical sources are allowed here, because it is not a `football-gear` page.

## Worth knowing, lower priority

- **Coaching drills by age (a coaching funnel, not affiliate):** football warm up drills 880 Jan/Mar (comp 1), football drills for 10 year olds 320, for U8 260, for 6 year olds 210-260. All peak Jan-Mar and Sep, with low competition. Natural siblings to `football-drills-for-7-and-8-year-olds`.
- **Indoor / futsal shoes:** futsal shoes, indoor football shoes and indoor football boots run 2,900 Nov and 2,400-2,900 Jan. Check whether `childrens-astro-turf-trainers` already ranks before adding a page; an indoor section may be the cheaper lever.
- **Football birthday parties (evergreen):** football birthday cake 3,600 (Jan 4,400), football birthday party decorations 720-880, party bags 1,300. Good Amazon basket, but Pinterest, Etsy and shop SERPs make it hard. Only worth it with a strong angle.
- **Women's World Cup 2027:** rises from Mar (5,400) to Apr (8,100) and peaks in summer. A social and promotion moment for the girls' football pages more than a new article. Plan an Instagram push for May-July.
- **Touchline parent winter kit:** heated gilet (Nov 40,500), heated socks (Jan 27,100), rechargeable hand warmers (Nov 22,200), heated gloves (Nov 49,500). The volume is huge, but these are generic retail SERPs we can't win. Only the parent-specific phrasing could work, and Keyword Planner returns no volume for it. A possible single Instagram/Reel "touchline survival kit" with affiliate links, not an SEO page.

## Ruled out

- **football cards** (22,200, flat) and **heated head terms**: retail SERPs.
- **football trophies** as a standalone page: shop SERP, so it is targeted inside #5 instead.
- **"kids ..." phrasings** (kids football kit, goalkeeper gloves kids, football fitness for kids): Google hides their volume, so they can't be sized here.
- **kickster** (40,500): an unrelated brand.
- **football training at home / drills at home**: 20-110 a month.
