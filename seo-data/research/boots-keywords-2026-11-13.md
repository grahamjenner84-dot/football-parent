## Difficulty is no use here

## Conclusions

1. The sub-type searches (infant, velcro, indoor, astro) are shop SERPs. A new guide page would not rank; cover them as sections and picks on existing pages for the long tail, not as targets.
2. The way in for boots is the "best ... football boots for kids" family, which is guide-friendly (4-5 editorial pages on page one), and where Google already prefers our girls page (GSC 8.7 for "best football boots for kids") over the kids page (16.1). The kids page reads as a sole-type explainer with three picks; the girls page is a picks page. Fix the kids page rather than add pages.
3. Guides also reach page one on the 27,100 head term (footy.com #5, FourFourTwo #6), so a strong picks page has a route to it later.
4. Wide fit: the generic (adult) wide searches are guide SERPs at 1,600 each; the kids page wins its own kids variants at 2-3. Secondary.
