## Stocking Fillers for Training and the Garden

### A pump with spare needles

A [ball pump with needles](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B01A425KQW?tag=footballpar09-21) is the least exciting thing in the stocking and probably the one that gets used most. Balls lose pressure over winter, and a soft ball bounces flat, runs short and does not fly when a child strikes it properly. The needles are the part that goes missing, which is why a pump that comes with five is worth a little more than one with a single needle.

### A deflated ball and a pump

Let the air out of a new ball and it folds small enough for a stocking. Put the pump in with it, and pumping it up becomes part of Christmas morning. Get the size right: in England a size 3 ball runs right through to Under 11, size 4 covers Under 12 and Under 13, and size 5 starts at Under 14. Our guide to [football sizes by age](/football-gear/best-footballs-by-age) has the detail.

The [Mitre Impel](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B09B28DHW6?tag=footballpar09-21) is a durable training ball sold in sizes 3, 4 and 5, so choose the size on the listing. It is cheap enough to be the ball that lives in the car boot or at a grandparent's house.

### Flat cones

[Flat disc markers](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B07KPKQD6M?tag=footballpar09-21) are what many grassroots coaches lay out at training, so a child recognises them straight away. They are better than tall dome cones in a garden because they do not blow over and are flat enough to step on. A pack of ten is enough for a slalom, a small pitch or two gates to dribble through. Our [football drills for 7 and 8 year olds](/coaching/football-drills-for-7-and-8-year-olds) has games that need nothing more.

### A size 1 skills ball

A [size 1 skills ball](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=size+1+skills+football&tag=footballpar09-21) is small enough to fit in a stocking fully inflated. It is for keepie-ups, toe taps and close control on the landing, and the smaller ball needs cleaner touches. It is not a substitute for a proper ball outside, so treat it as an extra.

## Matchday Stocking Fillers

### A boot brush that holds water

Grassroots football in December means mud, and mud left to dry on boots is far harder to shift by Saturday. The [Boot Buddy](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B014UPAHO4?tag=footballpar09-21) holds water in the handle and brushes through it, so the mud comes off pitchside before it sets.

<ParentNote>
It's really handy straight after the game to scrape the mud off and give the boots a little wash while the mud is still wet. It also makes him more likely to do it himself rather than relying on me.
</ParentNote>

### Sock tape

Older children copy the professionals and tape their socks below the knee to hold shin pads in place. A roll of [sock tape](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B07QQ1QMLV?tag=footballpar09-21) is cheap and gets used every match. The colour matters: under Law 4 of the Laws of the Game, tape worn on the outside of the sock has to be the same colour as the part of the sock it covers, so white tape on a red sock is not allowed. Check the club's sock colour before choosing.

Younger children whose pads slip are usually better served by a pair with an ankle guard and a strap, which our [shin pads guide](/football-gear/shin-pads/best-shin-pads-for-kids-football) covers.

### Grip socks

Grip socks have rubber pads on the sole that stop the foot sliding inside the boot. Many older players wear them under a cut-down match sock. A [3-pair pack of kids' grip socks](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0DT15P7DN?tag=footballpar09-21) is about £6, and this one is sold for ages 9 to 12. Ask the club first whether a cut sock is allowed in matches, as some teams want the full sock worn.

### A water bottle with their name on it

At a grassroots match a dozen identical bottles end up in a row by the pitch, and one often goes home with the wrong family. A [football water bottle](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=kids+football+water+bottle&tag=footballpar09-21) with a name written on it in permanent marker fixes that for a few pounds.

## Cold-Weather Stocking Fillers

Midwinter matches are hard on younger players. Cold hands are a common reason for a younger child to want to come off at half time, and the parents on the touchline suffer as much.

- **Gloves.** Thin football gloves with grip on the palm, so a child can still take a throw-in. Our guide to [football gloves for winter training](/football-gear/best-football-gloves-for-winter-training) covers what to look for.
- **A base layer.** Football skins under the shirt, but in a match the sleeves must match the main colour of the shirt sleeve. Our guide to [football base layers for kids](/football-gear/best-football-base-layers-for-kids) covers the colour rule and sizing.
- **Rechargeable hand warmers.** A [2-pack of rechargeable hand warmers](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0CG98CH4D?tag=footballpar09-21) goes in a coat pocket for the walk back to the car and the half-time team talk, and doubles as a present for the parent standing on the touchline. About £15 for two.

A snood looks like an obvious winter stocking filler, but snoods are not allowed in matches, and a referee can ask a child to take one off. Fine for the walk to the pitch or a cold training warm-up if the coach is happy, but not for a Saturday game.

## Football Stocking Fillers by Age

**Ages 5 to 7.** A foam ball, flat cones, a size 3 ball with a pump, and an annual. Card games suit children who can read the player stats.

**Ages 8 to 10.** Match Attax, an annual, winter gloves, a pump and a size 1 skills ball. This is when children start to copy what older players wear, so grip socks become popular; check the club allows a cut sock first.

**Ages 11 and up.** Sock tape in the club colour, grip socks, a base layer and a pump. Switch any new ball to a size 4 from Under 12. A spare pair of [goalkeeper gloves](/football-gear/best-goalkeeper-gloves-for-kids) suits any child who takes a turn in goal, since latex palms wear quickly on 3G.

## Check the Club Kit Rules First

Several of the most popular football stocking fillers depend on the club's kit rules, so check three things before buying:

- **Colours.** Sock tape and base layers have to match the kit in a match. Buy them in the club's colours, not the child's favourite colour.
- **Cut socks.** Grip socks only work under a cut-down match sock, and not every team allows it.
- **Snoods.** Banned in matches under the Laws of the Game, whatever the weather.

## Football Secret Santa Gifts for a Team

Plenty of grassroots teams run a Secret Santa at the Christmas training session or the end-of-year party, often with a limit of £5 or £10. With a squad of a dozen children, the safest gifts are ones that suit any player, whatever their size, position or club:

- **A football annual.** No sizing, and any child can use one. The Match Annual 2027 was under £5 when we checked.
- **A Match Attax or Topps pack.** Many children in an Under 9 or Under 10 squad collect one set or the other, and duplicates get swapped at the next training session.
- **Spare pump needles or a small pump.** Not exciting to open, but every family uses one.
- **Sock tape in the team's sock colour.** Because it matches the club kit, it works for any child in the squad.
- **A foam ball.** For the younger age groups, where a ball for the house goes down better than anything practical.

Avoid anything sized, like gloves, socks or base layers, unless you know who is buying for whom. If the parents are clubbing together for the coach as well, our guide to [gifts for football coaches](/parent-guides/gifts-for-football-coaches) covers what coaches use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are good football stocking fillers for a 10 year old?

A Match Attax multipack, a football annual, winter gloves and a pump with needles. A 10 year old still plays with a size 3 ball, so a size 3 training ball, let down and folded into the stocking with the pump, also works.

### Can you put a football in a Christmas stocking?

Yes, if you let the air out first. A deflated ball folds small enough for most stockings, and including the pump means it can be pumped up on Christmas morning. A size 1 skills ball fits without letting it down.

### What can I get a footballer for under £5?

A football annual, a few packets of cards, or spare pump needles. The Match Annual 2027 was under £5 when we checked.
