## How to Calculate Equal Game Time

*Draft: the Sheffield FA method in plain text: outfield positions times match length, divided by the outfield players available. A worked example (7v7, 50 minutes, 10 players, one keeper all game: 6 x 50 = 300 outfield minutes over 9 players, about 33 each). Then the goalkeeper variant: when the keeper rotates outfield, the whole squad shares the minutes in goal too, so the target rises. Source each number.*
