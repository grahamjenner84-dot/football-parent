## The maths nobody has time for on a Sunday morning

Fair game time sounds simple until you try to hold it in your head. Twelve
players, four quarters, nine on the pitch, and someone missed last week
through illness. Do it properly and you are working out shares of available
minutes on the touchline while a match is going on.

Most coaches end up doing it roughly, feeling guilty about it, and hearing
about it from a parent three weeks later.

## Set the rules once

Tell the app how you want game time shared out — equal minutes for everyone,
a minimum each, or a fixed lineup you adjust yourself. It applies that to
every match without you doing the arithmetic again.

Formations are drag and drop. Change your shape and the rotation reworks
around it.

## See who is owed minutes

Every player's share of available minutes updates the moment you save a
match. Not appearances, not games started — actual minutes on the pitch as a
share of what was available to them.

That is the number a parent is really asking about when they ask whether
their child is getting a fair go. Being able to answer it, from your phone,
with a real figure, changes that conversation entirely.

## It carries across the season

One unfair week is not a problem. Six of them is, and it is the kind of thing
that is invisible until someone adds it up. The season view does the adding
up for you, so you can put it right in week seven instead of finding out in
May.
