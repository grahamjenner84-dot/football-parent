## The maths nobody has time for on a Sunday morning

Fair game time sounds simple until you try to hold it in your head. Twelve
players, four quarters, nine on the pitch, and two who missed last week. Do it
properly and you are working out shares of available minutes on the touchline
while the match is going on.

So it gets done roughly, and the first sign it was wrong is a parent asking
about it.

## Set the rules once

Tell the app how you want game time shared out: equal minutes for everyone, a
minimum each, or a fixed lineup you adjust yourself. It applies that to every
match without you doing the arithmetic again.

Formations are drag and drop. Change your shape and the rotation reworks
around it.

## See who is owed minutes

Every player's share of available minutes updates the moment you save a match.
Actual minutes on the pitch, as a share of the minutes that were available to
them.

That is the figure a parent wants when they ask whether their child is getting
a fair go, and you can answer it from your phone.

## It carries across the season

Uneven weeks are hard to spot one at a time. The season view adds them up, so
you can put it right in November instead of finding out in May.
