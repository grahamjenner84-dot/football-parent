## Picking the team without starting from scratch

Every week begins with the same grid: the same squad, the same formation,
minus whoever has dropped out. The Coach App keeps all three in one place, so
selection starts from where you left off rather than a blank sheet.

## Formations that match your age group

Set your game format (5, 7, 9 or 11-a-side) and pick a shape. Positions are
drag and drop, so moving a player takes a thumb.

Change formation mid-season and your squad comes with you.

## Generate, then adjust

Tap generate and you get a lineup that respects the rules you set: who needs
minutes, who plays where, how many subs each period. It is a starting point,
and you can change any of it before you save.

## Availability before you pick

Send parents a link and see who is in, who is out and who has not replied. The
squad you pick from is then the one that will actually turn up, instead of
rebuilding the lineup in the car park on Sunday morning.
