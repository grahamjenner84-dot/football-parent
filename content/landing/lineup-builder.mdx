## Team selection is the job, not the admin around it

Working out who plays where is coaching. Rewriting the same grid every week
because two players dropped out is not.

The Coach App keeps your squad, your formation and your rules in one place,
so picking a team starts from where you left off rather than from a blank
sheet.

## Formations that match your age group

Set your game format — 5, 7, 9 or 11-a-side — and pick a shape. Positions are
drag and drop, so moving a player is a thumb movement, not a rewrite.

Change formation mid-season and your squad comes with you.

## Generate, then adjust

Tap generate and you get a lineup that respects the rules you set: who needs
minutes, who plays where, how many subs each period. It is a starting point
you then change, not something imposed on you.

Most coaches move two or three players and go. That is the difference between
ten minutes of admin and thirty seconds.

## Availability before you pick

Send parents a link and see who is in, who is out and who has not replied.
Pick from a squad you know is real, rather than building a lineup on Friday
and rebuilding it in the car park on Sunday.
