## Stats that come from the match, not from memory

Most grassroots stats are reconstructed on a Sunday evening from a scrappy
note and whatever anyone can remember. They are approximately right, which is
another way of saying wrong.

Log goals, assists and cards from the touchline as they happen — a couple of
taps, no clipboard — and the season table builds itself.

## What you actually get

Results and goal difference. Top scorers. Assists. Appearances. Minutes
played per player and each player's share of the minutes available to them.

All of it updates the moment you save a match, so there is no end-of-season
reconciliation to dread.

## Worth having beyond the trophy night

Knowing who has played where, and how much, tells you things that are hard to
see week to week: the player quietly drifting out of the team, the one who has
not had a run in their best position since September.

You can only act on that if the numbers exist.

## Built for one team, not a whole club

This is not a league platform or a club-wide system with an admin tier. It is
for the person on the touchline running one grassroots team, which is why it
works on a phone in the rain rather than needing a laptop and an evening.
