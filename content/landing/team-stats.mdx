## Stats logged while the match is happening

Written up on a Sunday evening from a scrappy note, a season's figures end up
roughly right at best, and there is no way to tell which parts have drifted.

Log goals, assists and cards from the touchline in a couple of taps, and the
season table builds itself.

## What you actually get

Results and goal difference. Top scorers. Assists. Appearances. Minutes played
per player, and each player's share of the minutes available to them.

All of it updates the moment you save a match, so there is no end-of-season
reconciliation to dread.

## Worth having beyond the trophy night

Knowing who has played where, and how much, shows you things that are hard to
see week to week: the player quietly drifting out of the team, the one who has
not had a run in their best position since September.

## Built for one team

The Coach App is for one grassroots team and the person running it, which is
why it works on a phone in the rain rather than needing a laptop and an
evening.
