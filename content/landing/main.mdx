## What It Does

### No Download Needed

The Coach App runs in your phone's browser, so you can sign up and pick your first team without going near an app store. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app, on iPhone and Android. If you'd rather install it, it's also on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=uk.co.footballparent.coachapp).

### Squad Management

Keep your full squad in one place, with an active/inactive flag for players who've stepped away without losing their history.

### Scheduling & Availability

Set up matches and training as one-off or weekly recurring fixtures, with location and notes, so the team always knows what's next.

### Team Selector, Lineups & Subs

Pick your starting lineup and bench on a pitch view, with drag-and-drop formations for every format from 4v4 to 11v11. Keep a fixed lineup, or switch on equal-time rotation and get a substitution plan for every quarter or half, so playing time stays fair without you doing the maths in your head.

### Live Match Tracker

Log goals, cards and substitutions as they happen, with a live feed your team can follow along to.

### Real Stats

Appearances, goals, assists and cards, calculated from what actually happened in your matches, not hand-counted after the fact. If you're currently doing this on a football team spreadsheet, this is what it looks like once it's automatic.

### Tournament Day

A dedicated flow for tournament formats, from lineup through to a live summary.

### Share With Your Team

Send parents, assistant coaches and team managers a link to join in and see the same fixtures and results you do.

## Fair Playing Time and Substitutions

Tell the app how you want game time shared out: equal minutes for everyone, a minimum per player, or a fixed lineup you adjust yourself. It builds the lineup and a substitution plan for every quarter or half, so nobody is working out minutes on the touchline while the match is going on.

Change your formation and the rotation reworks around it. Every player's minutes, and their share of the time available, add up across the season, so when a parent asks whether their child is getting a fair go, you can answer from your phone.

## Built for Grassroots

This isn't a club-wide platform for academies or leagues to run their whole operation. It's built for the person actually stood on the touchline: a head coach, assistant coach or team manager running one grassroots team, not a whole club's operation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to download the Coach App?

No. It works in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop, with nothing to install. Android users who prefer an app can get it from Google Play instead, and the same account works in both.

### Is the Coach App free to use?

Squad management, scheduling, team selection (including equal-time rotation and fair playing-time tracking) and organising a live match are free. Logging live match stats, full season stats and tournament mode are part of a paid plan.

### Is there an app for keeping track of playing time?

Yes. The Coach App plans fair playing time for a grassroots team: set your rules once and it builds the lineup, plans the substitutions for every quarter or half and shows each player's share of the minutes across the season. The team selector and equal-time rotation are free.

### Do parents and assistant coaches need to install anything?

No. Parents, assistant coaches and team managers you invite get a link to see fixtures, results and availability without installing anything or creating an account of their own.

### Does it work for tournaments, not just weekly matches?

Yes. There's a dedicated tournament day flow, from your squad and formation through to a live summary as group games move into knockouts.
