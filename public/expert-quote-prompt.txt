ABOUT THE SERIES
Football Parent features a guest expert (a coach, academy, physio, nutritionist, referee, etc.) answering questions parents actually ask. The post is co-branded with the guest, flatters them, and they reshare it to their own audience.

AUDIENCE
Each post is for ONE of two audiences, so pick one before choosing hooks:
- Parents trying to help their child improve and get noticed or into an academy (aspiring families, not ones already inside an academy).
- Grassroots coaches: sessions, player development, handling players and parents.
A hook is only worth using if that person would save it or send it on: specific, practical, or cutting against what they assume. General reassurance ("every child is different") and interview summaries are not hooks.

WHAT I WILL GIVE YOU
- The expert's name / handle and what they are known for.
- A long-form article, interview transcript, or set of full answers in their own words.

YOUR JOB
Read the whole source and pull out the 3 to 5 strongest, most parent-relevant hooks. For each hook produce a SHORT summarised question plus an answer drawn from what they actually said.

HARD RULES ON THE QUESTIONS
- The question is YOURS to write: a short, punchy paraphrase of what the answer addresses. It does NOT need to match any question in the source.
- Keep it to one simple line a real parent would worry about. "Should they specialise early?" beats "What is your view on early specialisation in youth development pathways?"

HARD RULES ON THE ANSWERS (most important)
- Every answer is the expert's EXACT words, copied from the source. Never reword, swap a word, reorder, fix grammar or add anything.
- You MAY cut: take one or two sentences, or drop a clause, and join the kept pieces with " … ".
- If the exact words don't make the point in 35 words or fewer, pick a different passage instead of editing.
- If a hook needs context the source does not give, drop it and pick another.

LENGTH (hard caps, shorter is better)
- Cover question 10 words, questions 9 words, answers 35 words (15 to 25 reads best), pull-quote 20 words, follow line 14 words.

HOOK SELECTION
- Favour the surprising, the reassuring, and the gentle myth-buster.
- Order the Q&A so the FIRST question is the punchiest, most scroll-stopping one — it may be used as the cover headline, so it must land the audience's real worry in one short line.
- Each hook should stand alone without the others.
- Avoid anything controversial, political, or that could embarrass the guest.

ALSO PICK
- The single punchiest line in the whole source to feature as the big pull-quote: exact words, cut only, same rule as the answers.
- A cover question: the angle as the worry the audience already has, in their words.
- A follow line: the reason this audience should follow Football Parent, as the end of the sentence "Follow @football.parent ...", so it starts with "for".
