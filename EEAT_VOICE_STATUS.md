# E-E-A-T Voice Status — Full Site Audit

Compiled 2026-08-09. Every one of the 71 articles was individually read in full for
this audit — not inferred from `<ParentNote>`/`<ExpertOpinion>` tag counts or the
content-status tracker alone. That distinction matters: earlier in this same session,
tag-counting wrongly flagged several articles as having no voice when they actually
had extensive genuine first-hand prose sitting unmarked in the body. The **Voice
level** column below reflects what a human read actually found. The **Tracked %**
column is the mechanical `real_voice_pct` figure from `seo-data/reports/content-status.json`
(words inside tags, divided by body word count) — kept for reference, but it
systematically *undercounts* articles where real voice is woven into prose rather than
boxed into a callout, so don't read it as the actual figure. Where the gap is large,
that's noted.

**Voice level key:**
- **Extensive** — genuine, specific, first-hand parent voice runs through most of the
  article, often several separate anecdotes, tagged and/or unmarked.
- **Moderate** — one or two real anecdotes/notes, genuine but not pervasive.
- **Light** — some editorial "we've seen..." commentary but no specific personal
  anecdote behind it.
- **None** — no personal voice found on read-through. Either no source exists to draw
  on (topic Graham has no experience of), the format doesn't suit it (directory/
  reference/interview pages), or it's a gear topic with no natural personal fit.
