# Girls' pathway interview: question set

Drafted 2026-10-08. Fills the **girls-pathway coach** slot, second in
`expert-question-bank.md`, and the weakest whole cluster on the site: eight
girls' pages and no pathway expert. The Girls United interview (2026-10-06)
moved three of them only to Light, because its answers are general grassroots
advice that never touch ETCs or academies.

Graham's friend at the FA is asking around, so the ten questions are grouped
by the kind of person best placed to answer them. One person may be able to
take all ten. If she knows two or three people, each takes their own group,
and the answers become one joint interview or separate shorter ones.

Ask everyone for real examples, anonymised where needed. Specific stories are
what make the answers count for E-E-A-T. FA or club staff may need their comms
team to approve being quoted, so check how each person wants to be credited.

## Group A: ETC coach or talent ID lead (ages roughly 8 to 14)

Someone who runs or coaches sessions at an Emerging Talent Centre, or does its
scouting.

1. **At an ETC ID session or taster for an 8 to 10-year-old, what are you
   actually looking for, and what do parents wrongly assume you want?**
   → emerging-talent-centres-explained (*How recruitment works*),
   girls-football-trials (*What Coaches Are Actually Looking For*)

2. **Walk us through how a girl actually ends up at an ETC: who puts her
   forward, what happens on the day, and how does the family hear back?**
   → emerging-talent-centres-explained, girls-football-trials (*What Triggers
   a Trial Invitation?*), how-girls-football-academies-work (*How Players Get
   Into The Pathway*)

3. **What tells you a girl will keep developing over the next two years,
   compared with one who's simply ahead right now?**
   → how-girls-football-academies-work (*What Actually Matters for
   Development*), emerging-talent-centres-explained (*What ETC Selection
   Really Means*)

4. **Tell us about a smaller or later-maturing girl you nearly overlooked.
   What made you keep her, and how did she get on?**
   → late-developers-in-girls-football (*The Physical Maturation Bias in
   Talent Identification*)

## Group B: Professional Game Academy coach (ages roughly 14 to 16 and up)

Someone at a WSL or WSL 2 club's girls' academy.

5. **Tell us about a player who joined your academy at 14 to 16 after being
   missed earlier. How was she spotted, and what convinced you?**
   → what-age-do-girls-football-academies-recruit (*Late Entry Into the
   Pathway*)

6. **What's the honest day-to-day difference between an ETC and a Professional
   Game Academy (hours, travel, school), and where do families misjudge the
   commitment?**
   → emerging-talent-centres-explained (*ETC vs Pro Game Academy*),
   girls-academy-vs-grassroots-football (*The Commitment Question*)

7. **Tell us about a girl who stayed in grassroots, or went back to it, rather
   than taking a pathway place. What made that the right call?**
   → girls-academy-vs-grassroots-football (*When to Think Carefully*)

## Group C: FA or county pathway lead (the national view)

Someone who works on the girls' talent pathway as a whole, at the FA or a
county FA, and saw the change from RTCs to ETCs.

8. **What changed on the ground when RTCs became ETCs, beyond the number of
   centres? What do parents still misunderstand from the RTC days?**
   → girls-rtcs-explained (*What This Means for Your Daughter*),
   emerging-talent-centres-explained (*Why ETCs replaced RTCs*)

9. **How does the girls' pathway differ from the boys' in how and when talent
   is identified, and what does that mean for a girl who isn't in it by 12?**
   → how-girls-football-academies-work (*How Girls' Academies Differ From
   Boys'*), what-age-do-girls-football-academies-recruit

10. **If a girl doesn't get in, or is let go, what should she do next, and which
    other routes genuinely work?**
    → girls-football-trials (*If She Doesn't Progress*, *Pathways Beyond
    Academies*), what-age-do-girls-football-academies-recruit
