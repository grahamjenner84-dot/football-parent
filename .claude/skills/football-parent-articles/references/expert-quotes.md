### 2. The "Golden Age" of motor learning

> "Biology dictates that the period between 5 and 11 represents the 'Golden

> "Far from delaying a child's ability to play as a unit, embracing the

### Small-group vs 1-to-1 private coaching

> My son doesn't do 1-to-1, he does small-group coaching, and it's worked
> well. I think 1-to-1 is best if there's something specific to work on,
> like first touch or ball striking, but a small group can help with that
> too. You share the cost, and there's more opportunity to practise skills
> under pressure. Get the right group and it can spur your child on. Get a
> couple of disruptive kids in there and they end up with less practice time
> than they would 1-to-1.

**Topics:** private/1-to-1 coaching, small-group coaching, cost-sharing.
**Used in:** [is-private-football-coaching-worth-it](/football-development/is-private-football-coaching-worth-it) (1/3) -
Phase 5B (2026-08-08).
